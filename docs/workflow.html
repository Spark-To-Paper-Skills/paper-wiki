Workflow Architecture

一张图看完整条流水线

The whole pipeline in one diagram

外层是知识回路——检索入库、逐篇精读、读成领域地图；内层是想法回路——立项审查、目标卡、进攻方向、便宜小实验、想法卡、查重法庭、加码闸、评级。过闸之后交给 Spark-to-Paper v2 跑实验出初稿，需要时再由 PaperJury 模拟评审。颜色按执行者区分，👤 处由人拍板。

The outer loop accumulates knowledge — retrieve, deep-read every paper, compile the notes into a field map. The inner loop spends it — scoping review, goal card, attack directions, one-night probes, idea card, novelty court, investment gate, tiering. Past the gate, Spark-to-Paper v2 runs the experiments and drafts, and PaperJury mock-reviews when needed. Boxes are coloured by executor; 👤 marks the calls a human makes. The diagram itself is in Chinese.

01 全流程 Full Pipeline

paper-wiki · 全流程

paper-wiki · full pipeline

一条把「读论文」变成「出论文」的流水线：👤 人拍板的决定 · 🔒 机器自动把关 · 📒 只增不删的档案。自动模式下 /wiki-auto 指一个文件夹，自动跑到「评级」为止；👤 处由机器代签并留痕、记进收件箱，人回来可翻案。

One pipeline that turns reading papers into writing one: 👤 human calls · 🔒 automatic machine checks · 📒 append-only records. In auto mode /wiki-auto takes a folder and runs to the tiering stage; the machine counter-signs the 👤 points, leaves a trace, and files it to the inbox — every call stays reversible when the human returns.

👤 标记的决定（值不值得做 · 豁免 · 判死 · 改核心设定）：人在场时人拍板；自动模式下机器代签、一律留痕，人保留翻案权。其余检查与记录全部由机器自动执行。
Decisions marked 👤 (worth doing · waiver · retirement · changing core assumptions) are the human's when a human is present; in auto mode the machine counter-signs them, always with a trace, and the human keeps the right to reverse. Every other check and record runs automatically.
整条流水线的原则：确定性分期付款——每一步只承诺当前证据买得起的那部分；大胆留给假设，严格留给验收。
The principle behind the whole pipeline: pay for certainty in instalments — each step only promises as much as the current evidence can afford. Boldness belongs to the hypothesis; strictness belongs to the acceptance test.
02 两个回路 Two Loops

外层攒知识，内层花知识

The outer loop earns knowledge; the inner loop spends it

图上的四种执行者颜色对应四类主体：paper-wiki 自己的环节（检索 / 精读 / 制图 / 发想 / 法庭）、执行实验的 Spark-to-Paper v2、模拟评审的 PaperJury，以及人。灰色是档案，不是 agent。

The four executor colours map to four actors: paper-wiki's own stages (retrieve / deep-read / map / ideate / court), Spark-to-Paper v2 running the experiments, PaperJury mock-reviewing, and the human. Grey is the archive — not an agent.

📚 外层 · 知识回路

📚 Outer loop · knowledge

检索按需拉新语料，没有定时器；每篇论文逐页精读成一张笔记卡，摘要、关键结论、出处可回查。每攒够一批，制图员把笔记读成领域地图：大家默认什么、哪里在打架、哪里挤满了、什么问题重要却没人解。

笔记库同时是查重法庭的比对语料——含外领域论文，按范围围栏标记内外。

Retrieval pulls new material on demand — no timers. Every paper is read page by page into one note card: summary, key findings, traceable sources. Once enough accumulate, the cartographer reads the notes into a field map: shared assumptions, live disputes, crowded corners, and important problems nobody has solved.

The same note library doubles as the comparison corpus for the novelty court — out-of-field papers included, marked in or out by the scope fence.

🧪 内层 · 想法回路

🧪 Inner loop · ideas

从好问题、文献缺口或意外发现三个入口进来，先过立项审查，写成只定问题不定方法的目标卡，再开粗糙的进攻方向；一晚上的小实验先登记预测再看结果。有了自己的证据才写正式想法卡，交查重法庭审理。

过了「查新 · 实据闸」才准加码：评级只分四档不打分，然后才轮到实验执行与投稿闸。

Three entrances — a good problem, a literature gap, or an accidental finding. Scoping review first, then a goal card that fixes the problem and the success criteria but not the method, then rough attack directions. A one-night probe registers its prediction before it looks at the result. Only with your own evidence does a formal idea card get written and taken to the novelty court.

Past the novelty-and-evidence gate, investment scales up: tiering sorts into four tiers without scoring, and only then come execution and the submission gate.
